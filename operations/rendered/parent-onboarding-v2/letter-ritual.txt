Hi there,

When I think about Letters, I imagine Reuben reading something we wrote while he was small and hearing the people we were then—not polished authors, just his parents trying to notice him well.

That sounds important, which can make it hard to begin. So do not start with a letter. Start with one sentence: “Today I noticed…”

Write one true detail you would want to send forward in time. Stop after that sentence if it feels complete.

Our Little World should never turn loving your family into homework. It should help you keep what matters, then get out of the way.

If it is helping—or still asking too much—reply and tell me. I am building this as Reuben's dad, and I want it to feel worthy of the families who trust it.
