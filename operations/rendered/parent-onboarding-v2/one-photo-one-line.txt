Hi there,

When my wife and I looked through photos of Reuben, we would stop on the same image and start telling each other what had happened around it.

One of us remembered the routine. The other remembered a look, a sound, or why we had taken the photo in the first place. The image held the scene; our conversation held the memory.

Try that with one ordinary photo from the last few days. Add the detail only you would know: what was happening just outside the frame, what had become part of the daily rhythm, or why this small moment stayed with you.

That sentence is not a caption for an audience. It is a note to your future family.
