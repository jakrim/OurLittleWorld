Hi there,

We built this because the photo was never the whole memory.

After my wife and I put our son, Reuben, in his crib, we would get into bed—and almost immediately start missing him.

We would open Apple Photos, watch the videos from that day, and keep scrolling backward: yesterday, last month, all the way to his birth. Sometimes an hour disappeared like that.

Reuben at eleven months—the little world that started all of this.

The photos brought the moments back. But they could not hold the things we were saying to each other while we looked: the details only we knew, what made us laugh, how a season felt, and what we hoped we would never forget.

I kept thinking there had to be a better way to keep the story around the photos. Then the idea grew: what if we built a baby company that could use the latest technology carefully to create genuinely wonderful experiences for parents—beginning with a private place for these memories? That is where Our Little World was born.

You do not need to rebuild the past tonight. Choose one photo that still makes you feel something and add the sentence you would tell someone who was not there. That is enough to begin.
