# Export and Lapsed Subscription Policy

Status: product policy for PRD J4.

## Lapsed Subscriptions

Memories are never deleted for non-payment. If a subscription lapses after the paid
period ends, the family space becomes a read-only vault:

- Saved memories stay viewable.
- Saved memories stay exportable.
- New uploads pause.
- Assistant photo discovery and calibrated auto-save pause.
- Reactivating a paid plan restores new uploads and assistant discovery.

The app must not imply camera-roll originals are deleted or managed by this policy.

## Export Scope

Exports include photos, videos, voice, letters, firsts, prompt answers, dates,
metadata, and chapter summaries when available.

Current preview limitations must be disclosed before paid trust copy relies on the
export promise:

- Playable video files are represented by video posters in the local preview.
- Voice recordings are listed as references in the local preview; full audio files
  are not included yet.
- Private share links and print fulfillment are not included in the local preview.

## Copy Rules

- Use parent ownership language: "memories are always exportable" and "saved
  memories stay viewable and exportable."
- Use the concrete lapsed model: "read-only vault."
- Do not call lapsed access export-only or promise a grace period unless the product
  policy changes.
- Do not claim full playable video or full audio export until those files are in the
  export artifact.
