# Copy Positioning Notes

Internal notes for web, App Store, onboarding, screenshots, and in-app copy decisions.

## Category Benchmark

Use category benchmarks only to understand the parent job: a baby book that gets made
without becoming another chore. Public copy must not name competitors, imply direct
comparison, or position Our Little World as a clone or replacement.

Public positioning should translate the category job into Our Little World's model:

- assistant capture finds likely camera-roll moments when photo access is allowed;
- prompts collect parent words in small, gentle questions;
- parents approve what gets kept;
- the private baby book grows from approved moments, firsts, letters, voice, and
  recaps.

Guardrails:

- Say "likely", "possible", "worth a look", and "you approve" when the app suggests
  memories.
- Do not claim certainty about milestones, feelings, dates beyond real metadata, or
  child intent.
- Do not overpromise background discovery where photo access, platform support, or
  trust calibration is not available.
- Do not say corrections "teach the model" unless matcher scoring consumes that
  signal.
