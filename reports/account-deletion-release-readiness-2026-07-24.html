Trust contract

The deletion system sees less than the product sees.

Never leaves the parent’s device before Keep

Unsaved candidates, Photos asset IDs, face evidence, fingerprints, selection rationale, rejects, unavailable originals, Tonight queue state, text drafts, and temporary voice paths are not inputs to the Edge function, audit ledger, provider cleanup, analytics, notifications, reports, or another caregiver’s device.

Service-only audit

The durable ledger contains request and family-role identifiers, timestamps, status, attempt/error code, legal-hold state, and aggregate provider counts. It has no public/authenticated policy and no content or paths.

Fail-closed mutation window

A family lock makes established writer checks false and blocks membership changes between inventory and finalization. If the lock expires or a role changes, finalization stops and re-inventories.

Legal hold before providers

A hold stops the request before any Storage, Stream, R2, Stripe, database, or Auth deletion begins.

No deletion analytics

The mobile flow sends no deletion event. Provider and release evidence stays aggregate and never contains account content, email codes, media identifiers, or family examples.

Old media sessions fail closed

The R2 Worker writes a content-free family deletion marker before erasure and checks it before original-media cache lookup. A still-valid short-lived token therefore receives 410 instead of a stale cached original.

Technical architecture

One resumable transaction across five systems.

1. MobileExport, server preview, OTP, exact confirmation.
2. Supabase DBAudit row, legal hold, role inventory, family locks.
3. ProvidersStorage prefix, Stream UID + family metadata, R2 prefix, Stripe.
4. FinalizerDelete sole families; remove shared/Circle membership; minimize retained records.
5. Auth + deviceHard-delete user; clear SQLite, AsyncStorage, drafts, notifications, session.

Why these technologies

Expo/React Native keeps the account journey inside the shipped family app and can erase native SQLite, files, notifications, and secure auth state. Supabase Postgres/RLS owns family roles, lifecycle locks, cascades, nullable authorship, and service-only audit. Deno Edge Functions provide the admin-only orchestration boundary without shipping credentials. Cloudflare Stream and R2 hold video/original media, so their deletion must be verified before the family rows disappear. Wrangler validates that Worker code and bindings bundle without mutating production.

Claims supported by the implemented behavior

  • Your camera roll stays yours; deleting Our Little World does not delete device originals.
  • You can export your parent-approved archive before deleting the account.
  • Leaving a shared family does not erase the other parent’s family history.
  • Private discovery work and drafts stay on the parent’s device and are cleared with the deleted account.

Withhold from public claims until deployed and signed-device verified

  • “Delete your account in the app” as a currently available store-build capability.
  • Complete Stream/R2/Stripe erasure in the production provider configuration.
  • Any promise of instantaneous backup/log expiry or deletion beyond app-controlled systems.
  • Any claim that Apple or Google subscriptions are canceled by account deletion.
