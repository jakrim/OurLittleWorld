What remains unproven

The release is not complete. Both registered physical iPhones were offline, and the approved smoke authentication capability was unavailable. The signed binary therefore has not completed the two-writer or real-device journey.
  • Separate parent authorship, role boundaries, Keep/Skip, text, voice, photo, video, retry, collections, context, correction, lapsed/Circle, and export on signed 1.1.11.
  • Signed Cloudflare playback, voice delivery, upload recovery, and a physical notification tap.
  • Offline, iCloud, backgrounding, process termination, and Low Power Mode.
  • A representative remote Supabase rehearsal; no non-production branch exists.

Account deletion remains a separate gap

Authorship preservation is not account deletion. The app still lacks an export-first deletion entry point, reauthentication, idempotent deletion orchestration, provider and push cleanup, storage cleanup, auth identity deletion, and safe partial retry.

  1. Add settings UI with export-first guidance and consequences.
  2. Add a deletion-request ledger and idempotent Edge orchestrator.
  3. Handle sole writer, co-parent, Circle, lapsed, purchaser, gift, and billing cases.
  4. Preserve shared authorship while deleting private/device/provider state in order.
  5. Prove interruption, retry, retention exceptions, and Apple compliance on a signed build.

What the product does for people

Our Little World is not primarily “another photo organizer.” Its strongest promise is relief from camera-roll overwhelm. It gives parents a quiet ritual: notice a small number of meaningful moments, decide what is worth keeping, and share only those confirmed memories inside one trusted family space.

Safe now

  • A private family timeline for photos, notes, Firsts, and letters.
  • No public feed, likes, or audience-building loop.
  • Parents decide what becomes a shared memory.
  • Private-before-Keep candidate review in the release candidate.

Still withheld

  • Public availability of Tonight, automatic collections, or grounded context.
  • Flawless large-library performance or perfect identity matching.
  • Complete account deletion or full-fidelity export of every video/voice asset.
  • Production-ready two-parent, playback, or notification behavior.
