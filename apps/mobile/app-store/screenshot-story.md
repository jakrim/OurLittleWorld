# App Store Screenshot Story

The iPhone 6.5-inch set is ordered as one parent journey:

| Order | Message | Real UI proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Likely moments; parents decide what stays | On-device photo discovery |
| 2 | One calm next step | Today |
| 3 | Capture it your way | Add intention chooser |
| 4 | Everything kept together | Our World |
| 5 | Keep the story behind the photo | Connected Moment detail |
| 6 | Day by day and month by month | Saved-day rail and timeline |
| 7 | Find moments again | Places |
| 8 | Private and exportable | Archive export |

Public copy stays within the product contract:

- Discovery is optional and on-device before Keep.
- Suggestions never become family memories without parent approval.
- Only parent-kept moments grow the shared family record.
- No public feed, likes, followers, or public archive link is implied.
- Printing remains a future optional extra; the current proof is the exportable
  family archive and private summary.

Every screenshot is labeled accurately: the story is a sample, and the real
family photos are used with parent permission. Raw app UI captures live in
`source-ui/iphone-16-pro/`; upload-ready composites live in
`screenshots/iphone-65/`. The original supplied photo files are not retained in
the repository.

Open `review/index.html` for the full-size review page or
`review/iphone-65-storyboard.png` for a compact contact sheet.
