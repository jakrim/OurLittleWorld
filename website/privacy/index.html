Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 22, 2026. Our Little World is a private baby book for families. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Our promise

A keepsake for your family, not a social network.

We do not sell personal information and do not use a public social feed, likes, follower counts, or an advertising algorithm.

Private family space

Your memories are intended for the family space you create and the caregiver you invite.

Selected memories

Photos, notes, milestones, and letters are saved when you choose to keep them in the app.

Service providers

Infrastructure providers process information only as needed to run authentication, database, storage, and app features.

Information We Collect

  • Account information, such as the email address used for one-time-code sign-in.
  • Family profile information you add, such as a child's name, birthday, and family invite details.
  • Photos, photo metadata, notes, milestones, letters, and other memories you choose to save in the app.
  • Device permission status, such as whether you grant access to Photos, Camera, or Microphone.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide the app's private family timeline, library, milestones, prompts, letters, and invite features. We do not sell personal information and do not use a public social feed, likes, or advertising algorithm.

Photo Library Access

Our Little World asks for photo access so you can build a private baby timeline and save selected memories. Photos remain private to your family space unless you choose to share or export them.

Service Providers

The app uses service providers for authentication, database, storage, and app infrastructure. These providers process information only as needed to run the app.

Data Sharing

Your memories are intended for your private family space. We do not sell your data. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the service, or with your direction.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You can choose whether to grant Photos, Camera, or Microphone access in iOS Settings. You can also contact support to request help with account or data deletion.

Children

Our Little World is designed for parents and guardians to create a private record about their family. It is not intended for children to create accounts without parental involvement.

Contact

For support or privacy requests, contact jesse.krim@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version should be published at the app's privacy policy URL.
